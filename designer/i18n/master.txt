420|Open <b>Checks</b>. The tool tests that each breaker covers the load and protects its cable, that voltage drop stays under your limit, that RCD protection is present where the standard asks for it, that breaker breaking capacity covers the fault level, and that Lutron module and zone limits (load, current, voltage, temperature) are respected. It also checks the incomer rating and cable, phase imbalance and enclosure heat. <b>Fail</b> means fix it before building, <b>Review</b> means look at it and decide, and notes are for information.
421|Open <b>Circuits</b> and press <b>Add circuit</b>. For each circuit enter a name, type, power in watts, power factor, phase, cable length, protection (MCB or RCBO) and curve. The tool sizes the cable, picks the breaker rating and works out current and voltage drop. <b>Auto-balance phases</b> spreads the load over L1, L2 and L3, and <b>Renumber</b> tidies the references. On a low-voltage board, the quick calculator at the top gives the breaker for a load straight away.
422|Open <b>Diagram</b> for the multi-line wiring diagram. Every live conductor is drawn from its phase bus and the neutral from the N bus. A dashed box marks breakers that switch the neutral, a triangle marks the RCD, and dashed lines show circuits switched by a Lutron zone. Each circuit shows cable, protection, load bar and status.
423|Open <b>Equipment</b> to browse DIN-rail control equipment from Lutron, Crestron, Control4 and MDT KNX (processors, interfaces, power supplies, dimming, switching and shutter modules, gateways) with a brand filter, and the breaker library (Schneider, ABB, Siemens, Legrand, Hager, Eaton, Chint). Press <b>Add</b> on a module to place it on the board. Back in <b>Circuits</b>, choose the module and zone (channel) a circuit is fed from. Each module also gets its own feed breaker. Press <b>Use</b> on a breaker series to make it the default for MCBs, RCBOs or the main.
