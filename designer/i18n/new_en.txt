1031|Sign in, start or import a board
1032|On the hosted version you start from your <b>Dashboard</b> after you sign in. Press <b>New board</b> to start from scratch, open a saved project, or press <b>Import a panel</b> to build a board from a photo of a built panel or from a PDF schematic or circuit schedule. Add up to four photos of one panel, or one PDF, then press <b>Read panel</b>. The reader lists every device it can see (breaker kind, rating, curve, poles, RCD, circuit name, load, phase, cable, make and model) next to your picture. <b>Check and correct the table</b>, then press <b>Create board</b>. The new board opens on <b>Circuits</b> and works like any other, so you can add or edit circuits and labels and export the schematic, bill of materials, PDF and Excel. Anything the reader could not see is left blank and flagged for you to fill in.

Also under Quantities you can add models, change a quantity or remove a model. The tool hands out addresses on each line (up to 64) and checks bus current against the bus supply.
