## Full description (Google max 4000, Apple max 4000)

DBoard Designer is a design workspace for electricians, panel builders, system integrators and electrical engineers. Plan a distribution board, check it against your standard, produce the drawings and share the job with your team, on your phone, tablet or computer.

DESIGN THE BOARD
• Lay out DIN-rail devices on rails: incomer, SPD, MCBs, RCBOs, RCDs, contactors, meters, power supplies and control modules
• Lutron DIN modules and DALI devices with zones, addresses and bus-current checks
• 230/400 V AC, 24 V AC and 12/24/48 V DC marine boards

CHECK IT
• Design current against breaker and cable rating, with grouping and enclosure temperature
• Voltage drop, phase balance, RCD expectations, breaking capacity and earth-fault loop impedance
• Clear notes on what needs attention before you build

DRAW AND EXPORT
• Panel layout, single- and multi-line diagrams and circuit schedules
• Bills of materials, PDF and Excel exports to send to clients and suppliers

IMPORT
• Take a photo of a built panel or upload a PDF schematic and turn it into an editable board

WORK TOGETHER
• Find other members, chat privately or in groups
• Share boards so colleagues can open their own copy
• Send photos and files up to 25 MB in a chat
• Report or block anyone who misuses the community

YOUR WORK ON EVERY DEVICE
• Projects are saved to your account and open on the web, Android and iPhone
• Available in English, Romanian, Italian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Vietnamese, Thai, Russian, Polish, German, Chinese and Khmer

DBoard Designer is a design aid. Always check designs against the regulations where they are built and have them verified by a competent person.
