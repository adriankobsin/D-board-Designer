Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

DBoard Designer (the website and the Android and iPhone apps) is operated by Wave AVI ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it and the choices you have. Contact us at adriankobsin@icloud.com for any privacy question or request.

What we collect

  • Account details you give when registering: first and last name, email address, country, professional occupation and, optionally, company. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and only a salted hash is stored; we never see it.
  • Your projects: the distribution boards you design and save to your account.
  • Community content: messages you send, files and photos you attach in chats, projects you share, groups you create or join, members you block, and reports you send.
  • Panel import files: photos or PDF schematics you upload to turn into a board.
  • Usage events: sign-ins, projects opened, created or exported, and tools used, linked to your account, so we can run and improve the service. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Feedback you send us.

How we use it

To provide your account and store your projects; to deliver messages, files, invitations and notifications; to keep the service secure and prevent abuse, including reviewing reports; to answer support requests; and to understand which features are used. We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.

What other members can see

Signed-in members can see your name, occupation, company and country in the Community member list, and when you join the same chat or group. You can hide yourself from the member list in Account settings. Your email address is never shown there. Messages, files and projects you share are visible to the people in that chat, group or share.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: hosting of the website and its server functions.
  • Resend: sending emails such as invitations and shared-project notices.
  • Anthropic: when you use panel import, the photo or PDF you choose is sent to Anthropic's AI service to read the panel. It is processed only to return the result to you.
  • Apple and Google: if you install the app from their stores, they process data under their own terms as part of app distribution.

Our administrators can see account details, project counts, feedback and the content of reports (including a copy of a reported message). They cannot see your password and do not read private chats unless a message is reported to them.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to data is restricted by per-user rules in the database, so members can only reach their own data and what has been shared with them. Chat files are stored privately and can only be opened by members of that chat, through links that expire.

Your rights and choices

In Account settings you can correct your details, hide yourself from the member list, unblock members, download a copy of your profile and projects, and delete your account. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise them.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Account settings, on the website or in the app. This permanently removes your profile, projects, messages and the files you sent in chats. See Delete your account.

Retention

Data is kept while your account exists and removed when you delete it. Reports about content are kept as long as needed to keep the service safe, without the reporter's or reported member's account once those accounts are deleted.

Children

DBoard Designer is a professional tool and is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes, and tell members about significant changes in the app.
