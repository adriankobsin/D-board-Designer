Delete your account

You can delete your DBoard Designer account and its data yourself, at any time, from the app or the website.

Steps
  1. Sign in, in the app or at this website.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Under Delete account, choose Delete my account, type your email address to confirm, and confirm.

What is deleted

Your profile, all your saved projects, your messages and the files you sent in chats, your group memberships, shares, blocks and notifications. This happens immediately and cannot be undone. Download your data first from Account settings if you want a copy.

What is kept

Reports about content may be kept to keep the service safe, without any link to your account. Projects that others saved as their own copies belong to them.

Can't sign in?

Email adriankobsin@icloud.com from the address you registered with and ask us to delete your account. We will do it within 7 days and confirm by email.
