Need help with DBoard Designer? Email adriankobsin@icloud.com and we will get back to you, usually within two working days.

Inside the app
The Help button at the top of every screen explains each part of the platform. You can also send feedback from the dashboard.

Common questions

I can't see my projects on another device. Projects are saved to your account. Check you are signed in with the same email address on both devices (Account settings shows it).

I didn't get the confirmation email. Check your spam folder, then use "Resend" on the sign-in page.

Someone is bothering me in Community. Use ⋯ on their name or ⚑ on a message to report or block them. Blocked members can no longer message you, invite you or share with you.

How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.
